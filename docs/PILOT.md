# KARTA pilot — 30 days, free, one page

**What this is.** A spend-control layer for agents that pay for things. Not a wallet, not a
custodian, not a token. Your agent asks; KARTA decides; the receipt proves it.

**Why you were asked.** You run agents (or a platform where others run them) that can spend money.
The failure mode is not a stolen key — it is a run of perfectly valid payments nobody budgets for:
41 000 correctly signed calls over one weekend from a pagination bug; a $940 report bought because
a scraped page said so; a subscription nobody approved.

## What you get in 30 days

| Day | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | One task envelope for one real workflow: budget, allowed payees, no subscriptions, expiry |
| 3–5 | The guard in front of your paid calls: `ALLOW / HOLD / BLOCK` with the rule id and reason |
| 6–10 | Kill switch drilled on a staging agent (stop ≤ 2 s), evidence pack produced |
| 11–20 | Two more envelopes; the numbers: blocked payments, prevented spend, false blocks |
| 21–30 | Written review: what held, what broke, what you would need before paying |

## What it costs

Nothing for 30 days. No revenue share, no lock-in, no logo requirement.
After the pilot: Team $99/month (India $49) or Enterprise from $25 000/year for white-label.
If the pilot shows nothing, you owe nothing and we say so in the review.

## What we ask in return

1. One honest paragraph: where it broke, or why you would not use it.
2. Permission to name you as a design partner **only if** you actually installed it.
3. One introduction to a team with a similar workflow — if you think it is worth their time.

## What we do **not** claim

- We do not promise "100 % safety". The guard is deterministic, but the policy is only as good as
  the envelope you write.
- We do not hold funds. Keys stay with you or with the gateway you already use.
- We do not have paying customers yet. The pilot exists to find out whether the product is worth
  paying for — the review will say what we learned, not what we hoped.
